Packing Essentials

Everything you need to prepare for your Philippines adventure

By ExplorePH Team | December 17, 2025

1. Clothing

Pack light, breathable clothing suitable for tropical weather. Include swimwear, comfortable walking shoes, and a light jacket for cooler evenings in mountain areas like Baguio.

2. Travel Documents

Ensure you have your ID, passport (if traveling internationally), tickets, hotel confirmations, and any necessary permits or reservations.

3. Toiletries & Health

Bring your personal hygiene items, sunscreen, insect repellent, and any medications you need. A small first-aid kit is always handy.

4. Electronics

Pack your phone, camera, chargers, power bank, and adapters. Consider a waterproof case for your phone if you plan on island hopping or visiting waterfalls.

5. Miscellaneous Items

Include reusable water bottles, snacks for travel, a hat, sunglasses, and a lightweight backpack for day trips.
